It's time to revive my blog by doing some new year's resolutions. Last half of the 2017 was really busy. Getting up to speed with my new job, helping my parents with their housing things and getting two new lovely cats to our family has kept me really busy. I've at least partially neglected my overall well-being and I want to fix that so here are my new year's resolutions for 2018:

* Get my life in to balance. Find the right balance between free time, work, rest and exercising.
* Based on the above: go to the gym at least once a week and do some home exercises at least three times a week.
* Write one blog post per day.
* Read at least one book per month.
* And last but definitely not least arrange more quality time with my loved ones :)

So what are your resolutions for 2018? :)
